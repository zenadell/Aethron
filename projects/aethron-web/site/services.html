Why Aethron

The same six promises on every project.

Whatever you build, the way Aethron works does not change.

01

Files you own

Every migration gives you plain files you can host anywhere. No platform, no badge.

02

Runs on your Mac

Your templates, sites and keys stay on your computer. Only AI requests leave it.

03

Browser checks

Every page is opened in a real browser before Aethron calls it done.

04

Any AI model

Use DeepSeek, Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic with your own key.

05

Undo everything

Every change keeps the version before it. One click puts it back.

06

Done in minutes

A small template takes about six minutes from link to finished site.
