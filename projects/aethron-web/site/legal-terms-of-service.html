You need an account to use Aethron. Give accurate details, keep your sign-in safe and tell us if you think someone else has access.

  • Each account is for one person.

Aethron is free during the beta. Any paid plans and their prices are shown on our pricing section, and we will tell you before any payment is taken.

What you are responsible for

You confirm that you have the rights to the templates, images, fonts and text you use, including any template you bought or were given permission to use.

4. Acceptable use

Do not use Aethron to break the law, infringe someone else’s rights or disrupt the service.

  • No templates you have no right to use.

  • No sites that impersonate a real person or business without consent.

  • No unlawful, hateful or exploitative content.

  • No attempts to overload or bypass the service.

  • No reselling of access without our agreement.

We may pause an account that breaks these rules. Where we can, we tell you first.

5. AI providers

Aethron sends requests to the AI provider you choose, with your own key. Their terms apply to those requests and to how they handle your content.

  • Your key stays in the app's settings on your Mac.

  • If a provider refuses or fails a request, Aethron tells you.

  • You can switch providers at any time in Settings.

Beta software

Aethron is beta software. It checks its own work, but it can still make mistakes, so review a site before you publish it and keep copies of anything important.

Changes and feedback

Features may change during the beta. If you send us feedback, we may use it to improve Aethron without owing you anything for it.

6. Aethron’s property

The app, the agents and the Aethron name and logo belong to Jomiez. These terms let you use the app; they do not transfer it to you.

7. Availability and support

We work to keep sign-in and updates available, but the beta comes with no guarantee of uptime. Most of the app works offline on your Mac.

Aethron is provided as it is during the beta. To the extent the law allows, we make no promises beyond those in these terms.

To the extent the law allows, neither side is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law, or your rights as a consumer.

You can stop using Aethron and ask us to delete your account at any time. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms.
