FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Aethron actually do?

It takes a website design you like, from a Framer or Webflow template, a live site or a screenshot, and turns it into a site that is yours: your brand, copy and images, no platform badge, and code you can host anywhere.

Do I need an AI subscription?

You need an API key from any provider: OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek, Anthropic or OpenRouter. You can save several and switch in one click. Spend limits stop a run before it costs more than you set.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and newer). Unzip the download and move Aethron to Applications. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open, because the beta is not yet notarized by Apple.

Where do my projects live?

On your Mac. Templates, projects and keys stay in your own Library folder. Only the requests you make go to the AI provider you picked.

Will the animations survive?

Yes. Aethron changes the words, images and links and leaves the design and motion as the designer made them. Every page is opened in a real browser before you get it.

How do updates work?

When a new version is out, an Update now chip appears at the top of the app. One click installs it and keeps your projects, keys and conversations.
